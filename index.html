STR ROI calculator

Estimate annual income, expenses, and returns with your own assumptions.

What goes into the estimate

Booked nights = 365 x occupancy. Stays per year = booked nights / average stay length. Gross annual revenue, also the revenue basis for platform fees, = booked nights x nightly rate + stays x cleaning fee charged.

Cleaner cost is an expense: stays x cleaner cost per stay. Operating expenses include that cost plus taxes, insurance, utilities, supplies, maintenance, management, and other expenses. Mortgage payments are excluded from operating expenses.

NOI = gross revenue - platform fees - operating expenses. Cash flow = NOI - annual debt service. Debt service uses a standard amortizing mortgage, or equal principal payments at 0% interest.

Cap rate = NOI / home value x 100. Cash invested is the down payment only, with $0 closing costs assumed for v1. Cash on cash return = cash flow / cash invested x 100. Revenue per available night = gross revenue / 365. A return with a zero denominator is shown as 0%.

Airbnb uses 15.5%, Vrbo new uses 12%, and direct uses 3%. Vrbo old uses 5% commission plus 3% processing on the revenue entered, a combined 8.00%. The old column assumes Vrbo handled payments.

How fees work

Airbnb: 15.5% host only fee

Verified from our own payouts on Oct 24, 2026. We apply the host fee to nightly revenue after discounts plus cleaning and pet fees.

Vrbo: booking date decides the structure

Bookings before Oct 29, 2026 use 5% commission plus 3% processing. The blended fee was about 8.29% on our reservations. Processing in this calculator applies to host charges plus taxes collected plus any refundable deposit entered.

Bookings on or after Oct 29, 2026 use a flat 12% commission. The 12% Vrbo commission covers processing on rent and host fees when Vrbo handles payments. If you use your own payment processor, your own processing cost still applies. Check tax and deposit handling in your Owner Dashboard. The rate follows booking date. Existing reservations keep the old structure.

Airbnb: 15.5% is the rate for most hosts. Some hosts pay 14 to 16%.

Vrbo: 12% is the US standard figure under the new structure. Regional rates run up to 15%.

The old Vrbo column on this page assumes Vrbo handled payments. Hosts who used their own processor, and legacy subscription hosts, paid differently.

Keep pricing in sync

Vrbo Host Terms clause 4.5.7 says hosts must offer Content and Host Ancillaries that are 'at least as complete, accurate, current, detailed, and favorable' as on their other channels. The terms define Content to include rates and discounts, and trade coverage reads that as rate parity in practice. This page shares general information only. Read your own Host Terms for the rules that apply to your listing.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Vrbo fee change?

Oct 29, 2026, by booking date. Earlier bookings keep the old structure.

Why do payouts differ for the same stay?

The platforms apply different host fee rates and processing structures to the same host gross.

Are cleaning fees charged a platform fee?

Yes. The fee applies to nightly revenue after discounts plus cleaning and pet fees in our verified math.

How do discounts apply?

Discounts apply to the nightly subtotal only. Monthly discounts apply at 28 nights, otherwise weekly discounts apply at 7 nights. The additional discount also uses the original nightly subtotal. Cleaning and pet fees are never discounted.

Did guests used to pay a separate Airbnb fee?

Under the old split fee, guests paid 14.1 to 16.5 percent of the booking subtotal and most hosts paid 3 percent. Hosts have moved to the single fee during 2026, and bookings made before a host moved keep the old split.

Will an audit get me more bookings?

No. There is no guarantee of more bookings.
